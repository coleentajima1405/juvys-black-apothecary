Warehouse Coffee - Mendiola

Tucked right in the bustling heart of Mendiola's University Belt, Warehouse Coffee offers a dark, industrial refuge from the daily academic chaos outside. With its moody ambient lighting, raw brick aesthetic, and high-octane espresso options, it serves as the ultimate sanctuary for late-night study sessions and solemn caffeine cravings. Whether you need a quiet nook to read ancient lore or a powerful brew to survive finals, this spot brews pure liquid revival.

Location Details
Address Mendiola St, San Miguel, Manila
Opening Hours 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM Daily
Price Range ₱110 – ₱220
Best Time to Visit Late afternoon to evening for study sessions
